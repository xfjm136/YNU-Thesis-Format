# YNU 2024 Undergraduate Thesis Writing Spec

Source: `关于印发《云南大学本科毕业论文（设计）写作规范》的通知-云大本科〔2024〕54号 (参考).pdf`.

Use this as the default governing standard for 云南大学本科毕业论文（设计） unless the user supplies a newer official file, college template, or department-specific rule. Treat this reference as a strict checklist derived from the PDF. Do not treat examples in the PDF as content to insert into a student's paper.

## Governing Basis And Scope

- The PDF is titled `云南大学本科毕业论文（设计）写作规范（试行）`.
- It applies primarily to Chinese-written undergraduate graduation theses/design papers. Foreign-language theses may follow it by reference unless their program has approved separate rules.
- The PDF cites `云南大学本科毕业论文工作管理办法` and `学位论文编写规则（GB/T 7713.1-2006）` as related bases.
- The paper must comply with academic and industry norms, have complete content, reasonable structure, rigorous logic, clear priorities, fluent writing, accurate expression, and no typos. These are content-quality issues: report them if visible, but do not rewrite.

## Length Requirements

- If the Ministry of Education national teaching-quality standard for the major specifies a thesis length, that standard prevails; the major lead should communicate it to students.
- If no major-specific standard applies, the main body generally follows:
  - Science/engineering: body not less than 8000 Chinese characters.
  - Humanities/social sciences: body not less than 8000 Chinese characters.
  - Foreign-language majors: body not less than 5000 foreign-language words.
  - Translation-practice report: translated text 5000-8000 Chinese characters.
  - Art/design/creative non-text outcome: submit a written explanation of at least 3000 Chinese characters; the title is self-drafted.
- Length problems are content/compliance issues. Report; do not pad text.

## Content Structure

- Required sequence: cover, title, originality declaration and usage authorization page, Chinese abstract, `Abstract`, table of contents, body, references, acknowledgement, appendix when needed.
- Body sequence: preface/introduction (`前言` / `绪论` / `引言`), main body, conclusion (`结论` / `结语`).
- Title: short, clear, and generalizing; avoid uncommon abbreviations/acronyms. Usually no more than 20 Chinese characters. Team projects must use subtitles so every student has an individual topic.
- Originality declaration and usage authorization page: use the university unified format; author and supervisor hand-sign after review and consent. Do not create or fake signatures.
- Chinese abstract: 300-500 Chinese characters, independent and complete, no tables, notes, or citations.
- Keywords: 3-5, common professional terms, ordered from broad to narrow, separated by Chinese semicolons, no final punctuation. The line starts with `关键词：`.
- English abstract and `Key Words：`: correspond to the Chinese abstract and keywords; English keywords use English semicolons and no final punctuation.
- References: only cited sources belong in `参考文献`; uncited but relevant readings may be listed in an appendix titled `书目`. Total Chinese/English references must be at least 10. Bibliography style follows GB/T 7714-2015.
- Acknowledgement: concise and factual; do not copy generic praise.
- Process attachments such as proposal, mid-term check, and guidance records use university unified forms and are submitted in the thesis system; do not force them into the main DOCX unless the user supplies that template.

Body content compliance to report, not rewrite:

- Preface/introduction normally explains background and purpose, topic origin, theoretical and practical significance, conditions for completing the task, planned measures/methods/steps and goals, and may explain the paper structure. It generally includes topic background/significance, research value/significance, literature review, research status, and innovation points.
- Main body is the core content: research, analysis, argumentation, and formation of academic views; it may include literature review and usually consists of headings, text, tables, or formulas. Data must be true and reliable. Natural-science papers require correct reasoning and clear conclusions; humanities/social-science papers require correct viewpoints, sufficient argumentation, reliable evidence, appropriate use of systematic/comparative methods, model or scheme design where appropriate, empirical/case analysis where appropriate, and suggestions/improvement measures based on analysis.
- Conclusion summarizes final research results and new overall academic views reached from the whole paper. It should be concise, precise, complete, and highlight innovations. It generally includes basic views, corrections/supplements/developments/confirmations/negations of previous views, main innovations and reasons, limitations, insufficiencies, and unsolved problems.

## Heading Systems

Choose exactly one system and keep it consistent. The second system explicitly permits `第一章` + `第一节` + `一、` + `（一）`; do not mark that combination as mixed.

| Level | First system | Second system | Third system |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `一、` | `第一章` | `1` |
| 2 | `（一）` | `第一节` | `1.1` |
| 3 | `1.` | `一、` | `1.1.1` |
| 4 | `（1）` | `（一）` | `1.1.1.1` |

Heading formatting:

- Level 1: 三号黑体, centered, no first-line indent, paragraph before 0.8 line, after 0.5 line.
- Level 2: 四号黑体, flush left, paragraph before 0.5 line, after 0 line.
- Level 3 and lower: 小四黑体, paragraph before/after 0 line. The PDF example shows third/fourth numeric headings indented 2 Chinese characters.
- `结论` is an independent chapter and has no chapter number in the official example.
- Chapters must be separated with page breaks.

## Page Setup And Body Typography

- A4 paper.
- Margins: top 2.5 cm, bottom 2.0 cm, left 3.0 cm, right 2.0 cm.
- Body Chinese font: 小四宋体.
- Latin letters, Arabic numerals, page numbers, and heading numbers: Times New Roman.
- Body line spacing: fixed 22 pt; character spacing: standard.
- Body paragraph before/after spacing: 0 line unless the paragraph is a heading or another special object.
- Body paragraphs: justified, first-line indent 2 Chinese characters.
- Chinese paragraphs use Chinese punctuation; English paragraphs use English punctuation and standard English spacing.
- Chinese characters must use standardized simplified Chinese characters from the official simplified-character list, unless the discipline requires otherwise.

## Punctuation, Terms, Numbers, And Units

- Chinese paragraphs use Chinese punctuation throughout.
- English paragraphs use English punctuation and normal English spaces.
- Citations in Chinese text follow the PDF's square-bracket convention; footnotes for explanatory notes use circled numbers.
- Terminology must be consistent throughout the paper. Use national-standard technical terms when available; otherwise use industry/common terms. Special-meaning or newly coined terms should be explained or footnoted. This is a content issue unless it can be detected as inconsistent formatting only.
- Unless otherwise specified, Gregorian centuries, years, months, days, times, counts, and measurements use Arabic numerals.
- Years must not be abbreviated: write `2019年`, not `19年`.
- Significant digits must be complete: write `0.50:2.00`, not `0.5:2`.
- Physical quantity names and symbols must be consistent.
- Use legal units of measurement issued by the State Council. Unit names and symbols follow international common notation.
- When not expressing data, Chinese unit names such as `千克` may be used.
- Physical quantity symbols, physical constants, and variables use italics; unit symbols use upright/roman style.
- Software flowcharts and source-code listings should follow software-document format and be placed as appendices.

## Page Numbers And Header

- Cover, originality declaration, and usage authorization page: no displayed page numbers.
- Table of contents, Chinese abstract, `Abstract`, and body all use separate pagination sections. Preliminary pages use uppercase Roman numerals `Ⅰ, Ⅱ, Ⅲ, ...`, footer centered, 五号.
- Body starts independent Arabic pagination. Body, references, acknowledgement, and non-independent appendices are included in the body-page-number sequence and use footer center as `第×页（共×页）`, 五号.
- Header starts from body page 1 only: 五号宋体, centered, full current level-1 heading text.

## Abstracts

- Chinese abstract and English abstract each start on a new page.
- Chinese title: `摘　　要`, two full-width spaces between characters, 三号黑体, centered.
- English title: `Abstract`, 三号 Times New Roman, centered.
- Abstract body: 小四宋体 / Times New Roman, fixed 22 pt, justified, first-line indent 2 characters.
- `关键词：` and `Key Words：` labels are bold.
- Chinese and English abstracts must correspond in substance; English translation should be fluent, grammatical, use accurate technical terms, and standard capitalization/punctuation. Translation quality is content; report, do not rewrite.

## Table Of Contents

- The TOC must be generated automatically from Word heading/outline levels.
- TOC title: `目　　录`, two full-width spaces, 三号黑体, centered.
- Do not leave a blank line between the TOC title and TOC entries; the PDF warns that such a blank line may be included in similarity checking.
- Include Chinese abstract, `Abstract`, headings through level 3, conclusion, references, acknowledgement, and appendices.
- Level 1 TOC entries: flush left, 小四黑体.
- Level 2 TOC entries: indent 2 characters, 小四宋体.
- Level 3 TOC entries: indent 4 characters, 小四宋体.
- Entries use dot leaders and right-aligned page numbers; TOC text and page numbers must match the body.
- In the PDF example, abstract, level-1 headings, references, acknowledgements, and appendices are 小四黑体; lower-level heading entries are 小四宋体; Latin letters and Arabic numerals use Times New Roman.

## Citations And Notes

- Use sequential numeric citations in first-appearance order.
- Citation markers use superscript square brackets such as `[1]`, `[2]`.
- For a complete sentence citation, place the marker after the sentence punctuation and do not add a space.
- Explanatory notes and citation notes that do not belong in references use page-bottom footnotes with circled numbers such as `①`.
- Do not put citations in any heading.
- Program-specific national/industry citation standards may be used only if the major has formulated and obtained undergraduate-school approval for that specialty. If the document visibly follows another standard, report the conflict instead of converting citations automatically.

## Figures, Tables, And Formulas

- Figures: caption below the figure, centered. Use chapter-based numbering such as `图 1.3`; no repeated or skipped numbers. One full-width space between number and title. Caption Chinese font 黑体, numbers/letters Times New Roman, 小四. Keep figure and caption on the same page.
- Figure drawings and symbols should be printed; if not printable, they must be clearly drawn/marked. Drawings should follow national/professional standards or common industry practice. Layout should be clean, reasonable, with clear lines and standard labels. Visual/disciplinary drawing quality is usually a report-only issue.
- Tables: caption above the table, centered; if there is no title, put only table number at upper left or upper right. Use chapter-based numbering such as `表 1.3`; no repeated or skipped numbers. One full-width space between number and title, and no punctuation in the table title. Caption Chinese font 黑体, numbers/letters Times New Roman, 小四. Table body Chinese font 宋体, numbers/letters Times New Roman, 五号, single line spacing. Keep table and caption on the same page.
- Tables should mark quantities and units as needed. Numbers inside tables should be centered and vertically aligned. Do not use `同上`, `同左`, or similar shortcuts for repeated adjacent values; repeat the value explicitly. This may require content changes, so report unless the user explicitly requests content cleanup.
- Formulas: use Word equation editor, own centered line, chapter-based numbering such as `（1.3）` at the far right; no dotted leader. Long formulas break before `=` or at `＋、－、×、÷`, with the operator at the start of the continuation line. Appendix formula number is `（A（n））`.

## References, Acknowledgement, Appendix

- `参考文献`: start on a new page. Title is 三号黑体, centered. No blank line between title and entries. Entries are 小四宋体 / Times New Roman, fixed 22 pt, justified.
- Reference entries must visibly follow GB/T 7714-2015. Typical examples in the PDF include books `[M]`, translated books, journal articles `[J]`, online sources `[EB/OL]`, administrative documents `[A/OL]`, dissertations `[D]`, newspapers `[N]`, collections `[C]`, and book/article chapters `[A]`. Validate obvious structure; do not invent missing bibliographic fields.
- `致　　谢`: start on a new page. Title is 三号黑体 with two full-width spaces, centered. Body is 小四宋体 / Times New Roman, fixed 22 pt, justified, first-line indent 2 characters.
- Appendix: title follows level-1 heading format. Multiple appendices use `附录 1`, `附录 2`, etc.

## Automation Boundary

Safe to fix automatically:

- Page setup, margins, fonts, spacing, alignment, first-line indents, heading styles, TOC styles, page/page-section breaks, automatic TOC creation, field refresh, header/footer visual formatting, figure/table/formula alignment and fonts.

Report but do not silently rewrite:

- Missing abstract/acknowledgement/reference content.
- Wrong or mixed visible heading-number text.
- Reference entries without visible `[n]` numbers.
- Citation order/content mismatches.
- Keyword term list, punctuation, or translation if fixing would change visible words.
- Figure/table/formula numbers or captions when the visible text must change.
- Typos, expression quality, abstract translation, terminology choice, missing content, and word-count padding.
